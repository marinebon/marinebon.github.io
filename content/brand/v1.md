One identity, three places it shows up: the website (these CSS tokens), slides, and posters. Everything below derives from the website tokens so that the three stay in step. When a token changes on the site, change it here and in the PowerPoint theme.

## Palette

Cobalt is the brand (logo, buttons); teal is the interactive accent (links, active state); coral is reserved for the single call to action on a page or slide; sun highlights data. Dark surfaces use navy and abyss, never pure black. Let one color dominate, use one accent, and keep coral rare.

## Typography

<div class="type-sample">
<p class="m">Label · IBM Plex Mono · 12px · letter-spacing 0.14em · uppercase</p>
<p class="d">Space Grotesk for display: observing life in the sea</p>
<p class="b">IBM Plex Sans for body and UI. Humanist, scientific heritage, highly legible at small sizes. Use weight 400 for running text, 600 for lead-ins, and italic sparingly.</p>
<p class="m" style="text-transform:none;letter-spacing:0">IBM Plex Mono for data: 24.55°N 81.78°W · AphiaID 1080 · ES(50)</p>
</div>

- **Display** Space Grotesk 400–700: page titles, section heads, card titles, big numerals.
- **Body / UI** IBM Plex Sans 400–700 (+ italic): paragraphs, lists, buttons, captions.
- **Data** IBM Plex Mono 400–600: coordinates, identifiers, taxonomy tags, small labels.
- **Scale** 12 · 14 · 16 · 18 · 22 · 28 · 36 · 48 · 64 · 84 px (`--text-xs` to `--text-5xl`). Line height 1.05 for display, 1.5–1.65 for body.
- All three are open (OFL) and load from Google Fonts on the site. For slides and posters install them locally: [Space Grotesk](https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk/releases) · [IBM Plex](https://github.com/IBM/plex/releases).

- Cobalt wordmark on white or foam; white wordmark on navy, abyss or photography. No other recolors.
- Clear space of at least the height of the "M" on all sides; minimum width 120 px on screen, 1.5 in (38 mm) in print.
- The wordmark carries the full name, so do not repeat "Marine Biodiversity Observation Network" next to it.

## Motifs

Two motifs carry across the site, slides and posters. Use them instead of decorative bars or stripes.

**The pipeline.** Place-based indicators are composed from four configurable elements; show them as four chips in this order and these colors.

<ul class="chips">
<li style="background:#6d4aa7">dataset</li>
<li style="background:#01375f">place</li>
<li style="background:#15788f">method</li>
<li style="background:#f2683f">delivery</li>
</ul>

**Cards.** Content sits in white or foam-100 cards with 10–16 px radius, a 1 px `line-200` border or a soft shadow, and a mono label on top (the `Label` style above). Never an accent stripe along an edge, never a line under a title.

- **Slide** 16:9, 13.33 × 7.5 in. Title 30–36 pt Space Grotesk bold in navy; mono label 11 pt teal above each block; body 14–16 pt IBM Plex Sans; 0.5 in margins. One message per slide; the all-hands one-slider uses the three blocks *Overall goal · Recent successes · Recent challenges*.
- **Poster** 24 × 36 in portrait (2 × 3 ft). Title 90 pt, section heads 48 pt, body 26–30 pt, captions 20 pt, readable from 1.5 m. Navy header band with the white wordmark, white body with foam cards, QR codes 2 in square in the footer.
- **Templates** [mbon-template_v1.pptx](/brand/v1/mbon-template_v1.pptx) (16:9 title, section, content and two-column layouts) · [mbon-poster-template_v1_24x36.pptx](/brand/v1/mbon-poster-template_v1_24x36.pptx) (poster frame with header, card grid and footer). Both carry the theme above; the fonts must be installed on the machine that opens them.
- **Figures** use the facet palette for categories, `viridis` for continuous values (as in the H3 maps), and label axes in IBM Plex Sans with `line-200` gridlines.

## Changing the brand

Edit `static/css/tokens/colors.css` or `fonts.css`, rebuild (`hugo --gc --minify`), then update this page and regenerate the templates. Bump to `/brand/v2/` for any change that would make existing slides look off-brand; keep v1 online until the decks that use it are retired.
