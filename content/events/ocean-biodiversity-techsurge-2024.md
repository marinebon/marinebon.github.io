The Ocean Biodiversity TechSurge brought the Marine Technology Society (MTS) Ocean Enterprise
Initiative, NOAA IOOS, MBON and the National Oceanographic Partnership Program (NOPP) together
to ask what technology and information the ocean economy needs from biodiversity observing.
Its outcomes were written up in a report and in *A Prosperous Economy Requires Ocean
Biodiversity Information, Technology, and Innovation* in the Marine Technology Society Journal.
