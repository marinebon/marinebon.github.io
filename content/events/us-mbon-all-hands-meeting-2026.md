The US MBON All-Hands Meeting brings the national network's teams, working groups and partners
together at **The Edge at USF** in St. Petersburg, Florida, with hybrid participation.

**Goals**

- Reach agreement on key messages about MBON national goals and the MBON strategic framework,
  share updates on data management (helpdesk and product highlights; data to support fisheries),
  and consider future planning.
- Strengthen links among US MBON teams and partners, and the connectivity across efforts.
- Encourage common activities and outputs: technology applications, data sharing, product
  development and stories that show the network's value.

**Day 1 (Wed 14 Oct)**: MBON team-lead updates (one speaker per project and working group);
the MarineGEO strategy and MBON partnership; MoveBON; the Ocean Biodiversity Impact Initiative
and biodiversity KPIs; global engagement (AIR Centre, WCMB, GOOS, GEO); national goals and
strategic framing; Sanctuary Watch; and the Communications working group, including this website.

**Day 2 (Thu 15 Oct)**: MBON data contributions to support fisheries, with OBIS and GBIF
capabilities shown on a species of interest; linking the Animal Telemetry Network and MBON to
inform conservation and fisheries management; afternoon field trip to Egmont Key.

**Day 3 (Fri 16 Oct)**: the IOOS Marine Life Map; remote sensing and seascapes applications
(NASA program update, PACE, animal tracking, dashboards, eDNA and plankton imaging, foundation
models); eDNA in MBON (FAIR eDNA data tools, portals, a cross-node pilot of eDNA-based EBVs);
and MBON business: next steps on national goals, species distribution modeling, and a special
issue.
