The agreement between MBON, AIR Centre and the Azores Regional Fund for Science and Technology was first 
celebrated in November 2018. It established a partnership that supports the advancement of scientific knowledge and the creation of technology-based solutions that contribute to the conservation of marine biodiversity and ecosystems, as well as to the development of effective management policies at local, Atlantic, and global scales.
