A University of Maine Pelagic Fisheries Lab expedition, supported by [MBON/BioTrack](/working-groups/biotrack/), tagged seven white marlin off the mid-Atlantic shelf edge in September 2026. Five real-time SPOT tags and two pop-up archival tags are revealing migration routes; three marlin had already logged at least 1,300 nautical miles toward the Caribbean by early November. The tracks help identify offshore habitats and biodiversity hotspots for marine management.
