The **Marine Sensitivity Toolkit** catalogs species distribution models from many sources
(80,791 models from 11 sources, merged into 17,763 U.S. taxon surfaces, including AquaMaps),
describes them with [STAC-SDM](/tools/stac-sdm/), and serves them as cloud-optimized GeoTIFF
and GeoParquet alongside species sensitivity scores for offshore planning.
