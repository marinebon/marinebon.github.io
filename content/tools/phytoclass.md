**phytoclass** is a pigment-based chemotaxonomic method: it uses non-negative matrix
factorization and simulated annealing to estimate the biomass of phytoplankton classes from
HPLC pigment concentrations. The Southeast US MBON uses it to turn pigment samples from the
Florida Keys National Marine Sanctuary cruises into taxonomic occurrence data. A Shiny
graphical interface makes the method usable without writing R.
