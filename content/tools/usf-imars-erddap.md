The University of South Florida's Institute for Marine Remote Sensing (IMaRS) serves its
satellite products on ERDDAP. These include MODIS-Aqua, VIIRS-SNPP and Sentinel-3 OLCI ocean
color composites and means for the Gulf and South Florida, PACE-OCI hyperspectral reflectance
and biogeochemistry, VGPM net primary productivity and IMERG monthly precipitation. They feed
the sanctuary dashboards and [erddap-places](/tools/erddap-places/).
